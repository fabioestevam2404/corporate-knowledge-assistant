1. Purpose
This sample policy describes baseline rules for remote work eligibility, used only
as placeholder content to exercise the ingestion and retrieval pipeline during
development. It is not a real corporate policy.

2. Eligibility
Employees in good standing may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to
manager approval and role requirements.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and VPN access. Personal devices must comply with the
security baseline described in the companion Information Security Policy sample.

4. Working Hours
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, defined locally by
each team, and must attend scheduled synchronous meetings.
