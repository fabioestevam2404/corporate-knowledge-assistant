1. Purpose
This sample policy is placeholder content used to exercise the ingestion, retrieval
and access-control pipeline during development. It is not a real corporate policy.

2. Password Requirements
Accounts must use unique passwords of at least twelve characters, stored only as
salted hashes, never in plain text or logs.

3. Access Control
Access to systems and documents follows least-privilege and role-based access
control. Confidential material additionally requires an explicit access grant.

4. Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team immediately
through the designated incident channel.
